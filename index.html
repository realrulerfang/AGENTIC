FAQ

 

Does the agent hold my keys?

No. It prepares transactions and you sign them in your own wallet.

Which network?

Elysium, the Hyperliquid-aligned L2 by Kinetiq. Gas is paid in HYPE.

Is this live?

The demo above is simulated. The mainnet integration depends on Elysium’s launch, planned for November 9.

Can it make mistakes?

Yes. That is why it simulates first, enforces limits and asks for your approval.
